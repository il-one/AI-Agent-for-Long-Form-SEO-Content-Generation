### 2. Style Design Language
- **Visual Aesthetic**: "Premium Editorial" – think high-end publications like The Atlantic or Medium. The design should feel sophisticated, trustworthy, and airy.
- **Color Palette**: Use a "Refined Professional" scheme. 
    - Background: Soft off-white (#FAFAFA) or very light grey to reduce eye strain.
    - Typography: Deep Charcoal (#2D2D2D) for high contrast and readability.
    - Accents: A single, bold accent color (e.g., Deep Navy, Forest Green, or Burnt Orange) for links, bullets, and borders.
- **Typography**: 
    - Headings: Use a bold, high-contrast Serif font (e.g., Playfair Display or Lora) to convey authority.
    - Body Text: Use a clean, highly legible Sans-Serif font (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or Roboto) at a comfortable size (18px-20px).
- **Spacing & Whitespace**: Implement a "Breathable" layout. Use wide margins (max-width of 800px for the text column) and significant vertical padding between sections to emphasize the "SEO Long-form" nature of the content without overwhelming the reader.

### 3. Component Guidelines
- **Callout Cards**: Use subtle background tints or left-border accents to highlight "Authoritative Advice" or "Key Tips" woven throughout the sections.
- **Responsive Design**: Ensure the article scales perfectly for mobile devices. On smaller screens, the font size should adjust slightly, and the table of contents should transform into a collapsible menu.
- **Interactive Elements**: Add a smooth scroll effect for the Table of Contents links and a subtle hover state for any interactive bullets or call-to-action buttons.
